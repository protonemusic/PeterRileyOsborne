Import Sports & Coaches

Paste a list with a season heading (Fall, Winter, Spring) above its sports and a "Head Coach:" line under each sport. A coach line can include an email address. Sports, seasons and coaches already in the app are not added twice, so it's safe to import the same list again.

Each student gets the next day of the program every day they sign in, starting at Day 1. Missing more than 5 days the weight room was open starts them over at Day 1. After the last day they see "Program complete" until a new program is made active.

Upload a Program

PDF or Word (.docx). Each workout needs a heading like Monday: Weights above its table; weights rows need a "sets x reps" column (e.g. 5 x 3), running rows a plan (e.g. 4 x 40 yd). Workouts are used in the order they appear.
